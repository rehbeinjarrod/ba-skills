# Untangle the rules

When: two conditions bear on one step, or two rules give different answers for the same case.

Steps:

1. Put each condition as a yes or no question, in the person's words.
2. List every combination: two conditions give four cases, three give eight.
3. For each case ask: "What happens here, and who decides?"
4. Cases with the same answer are one branch. Each different answer is a `Go to` with its condition.
5. A case the person says cannot happen is struck, with their reason.

Stop when: every case has an answer, or is a Question naming who can give one. With four conditions or more, start with the two that matter most.

Output: one decision step with a `Go to` for each answer, a Requirement for each rule, an example for each case.
