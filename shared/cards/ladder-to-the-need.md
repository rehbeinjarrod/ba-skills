# Ladder to the need

When: the person names a screen, a system or a fix, and not what someone needs.

Steps:

1. Keep their words for what they asked for.
2. Ask: "What would that let them do that they cannot do today?"
3. Ask the same of each answer, three times at most, until it names a result for a named person.
4. Say that result back as a Requirement's statement. Ask if it is right.
5. Ask: "If that happened another way, would it do?" On "yes", what they first asked for goes under `### Left to engineering`.

Stop when: the statement stands without the solution in it, or the person says the solution is required. Then its `Source` is who requires it.

Output: one Requirement stated as a need, and where the solution went.
