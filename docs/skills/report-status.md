# report-status

**Reach for it when** you want to know where a piece of work stands, what to do next, or who you are waiting on, with messages to chase them. To record an answer that came back, use [capture-requirements](capture-requirements.md). For an approver's yes, [prepare-signoff-brief](prepare-signoff-brief.md).

**Say** "Where are we?" Then "chasers" for the messages, or "all" for the full list of next steps. When a message has gone, or you have asked someone yourself, say so.

**What comes back first** is the work's name and size, one sentence on what is settled, the next three things to do, who you are waiting on and for what, and what has changed since the last approved version.

**How to tell it is working**

- Every count, name, date and age in the reply is taken from your records, not estimated.
- Each chaser is addressed to one person, lists everything they owe and says what it holds up.
- Your records are as they were, but for an `Asked` date on each question you said you had asked.

**It will not** send anything. The chasers are ready to paste, and they go from you. The one thing it writes is that `Asked` date.

**Good to know**

- A chaser goes to the person who can answer, never to whoever raised the question.
- A question you have asked is waiting on a reply: it drops out of what to do next and of the chasers until it is answered. To chase it again, delete its `Asked` line.
- Three kinds of thing are owed: an answer to a question, confirmation of an assumption, and a sign-off.
- A question holds things up until someone gives a working answer, so "waiting on" says which features each one holds up.
