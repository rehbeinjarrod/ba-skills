# adopt-house

**Reach for it when** you want briefs to read like your organisation's own documents (its words, headings and layout), or you want to set where briefs and tracker items are sent. Every other skill works without it, in a plain default style.

**Say** "Adopt our house from these documents" and give two or three documents your organisation has already approved.

**What comes back first** is your house, drafted: your words with the document each came from, the layout found, what was assumed, what is still not set, and a sample brief made through it.

**How to tell it is working**

- The sample brief uses your headings and your words for things.
- Each of your words names the document it came from.
- After you say "use it", every later brief comes out in that form.

**It will not** change what your records say: a house changes how a brief reads, never its content. It does not guess a place to send things.

**Good to know**

- Every place comes from you, as a link. Before a place is stored, a test page and a test item are sent there for you to look at, and it is kept only on your yes.
- A house is set up once and shared by a team. It holds the boards. The page a piece of work's documents are created under is held with that work's records, so each piece of work names its own.
- Without a connection to your wiki and tracker, briefs are left in your folder for you to place by hand.
