# prepare-signoff-brief

**Reach for it when** you want something to send to approvers, or an approver has replied. A reply that asks for a change goes to [capture-requirements](capture-requirements.md).

**Say** "Prepare the sign-off brief." Later, "send it", or "Priya replied: approved".

**What comes back first** is the brief, with what approvers are asked to approve and a "Still open" list: unanswered questions, records still in draft, examples to confirm and assumptions awaiting their owner. If your records hold a process, it first asks which form you want, unless you named one: a priority table or a process map.

**How to tell it is working**

- Every requirement in the brief is word for word from your records.
- After a yes, your records hold the approver's name, the date, their own words and which version of the records they approved.

**It will not** hold a brief back because things are open: it makes one whenever asked and says what is open. Nor will it record a yes without a name, a date and the approver's words.

**Good to know**

- Your request to send is the yes: it does not ask again.
- Before a brief is sent the records are frozen, so a yes names exactly what was approved. A later brief lists what has changed since and asks for a yes to those changes only.
- A yes counts however it reached you: in conversation, by email or as a comment on the page.
- After a change to something approved, whether it goes back for sign-off is your call. Status says what changed; regrouping into features never reopens an approval.
- The page sent is a copy: an edit made on it by hand is lost at the next sending.
