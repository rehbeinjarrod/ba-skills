# ask-records

**Reach for it when** you have a question about a piece of work: what was agreed, what a feature covers, what is out of scope, who owes an answer. For the whole picture, use [report-status](report-status.md). To add new material, [capture-requirements](capture-requirements.md).

**Say** the question itself: "What did we agree about refunds after the class has started?"

**What comes back first** is a short answer, then "From the records": the words of each record behind it and who said them, then "Not in the records": what is not settled and who it waits on, and what nothing covers.

**How to tell it is working**

- Every statement names the record it came from and the person who said it.
- When the records are silent the reply says "The records do not say", and offers to record your question.
- Your records are unchanged unless you accept that offer.

**It will not** answer from general knowledge of the trade, or pass off a draft, a suggested example or a working answer as settled. Each is labelled as what it is.

**Good to know**

- The records are the only source.
- Something withdrawn is history: the answer comes from what stands, and says what was withdrawn.
- Where the approved wording differs from today's, it shows the approved wording and who approved it.
- A question you let it record then appears in status.
