# capture-requirements

**Reach for it when** you have notes, a transcript, an email or a document about a piece of work and want it turned into requirements, process steps and open questions. A correction, an answer that came back, or what a system does today is recorded here too. With nothing written yet, [scope-work](scope-work.md) asks the questions.

**Say** "Capture the requirements from these notes", and paste or attach them.

**What comes back first** is a draft, in the same turn: each requirement in the speaker's words with its examples, any process as numbered steps, the assumptions made, the size, and the three questions whose answers would change most.

**How to tell it is working**

- `work.md` in your folder holds a record for every point in your notes, marked draft.
- After you say "confirm", the records say confirmed.

**It will not** choose between two statements that conflict (it records a question), design the solution, or treat what a system does today as a requirement until a named person says it is intended.

**Good to know**

- An example the assistant suggested shows "To confirm" until you confirm it.
- An assumption is a question with a working answer and an owner, so it can be chased.
- Size never holds up a first draft. Small: one approver, one team, no regulation or contract, no data to move. Medium: any of those four is a yes. Large: the goal needs several features.
- A defect is something a system does that departs from a requirement already agreed. Called a defect with no such requirement, it is recorded as a question (what should happen?) and gets its verdict once that is agreed.
- Confirmed is your word. Approved is an approver's.
