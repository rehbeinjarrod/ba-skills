# Which skill do I reach for?

Nine skills, each for one job a business analyst does. Say what you want in your own words and the assistant picks the skill; the pages below tell you what to expect from each, and how to tell it is working.

| I want to... | Reach for |
|---|---|
| turn meeting notes, a transcript or a document into requirements, process steps and open questions | [capture-requirements](capture-requirements.md) |
| be asked the right questions to pin down what a piece of work is, before much is written down | [scope-work](scope-work.md) |
| work through one feature in detail: who does what, and what happens when it goes another way | [define-feature](define-feature.md) |
| group the requirements into features and give engineers something to start from | [slice-requirements](slice-requirements.md) |
| send something for approval, or record that an approver said yes | [prepare-signoff-brief](prepare-signoff-brief.md) |
| say when it will be delivered, and show progress against that | [forecast-delivery](forecast-delivery.md) |
| see where the work stands, what to do next and who I am waiting on, with messages to chase them | [report-status](report-status.md) |
| check what was agreed, what a feature covers or who owes an answer | [ask-records](ask-records.md) |
| make the briefs look like my organisation's own documents, and set where they are sent | [adopt-house](adopt-house.md) |

## A usual order

There is no setup step and no fixed order. A common path is: capture, then sign-off; for more than a small job, slice and hand over as well; for a large one, start with scope and add a forecast. Status and questions are for any time.

## Three things true of every skill

- Everything known about a piece of work is kept as records in one plain text file, `work.md`, in the folder you are working in. You can read it and edit it yourself.
- What a person said is kept apart from what the assistant added. Anything the assistant added is marked until you confirm it.
- The skills record what the business needs and why. How it is built is left to the engineers.
