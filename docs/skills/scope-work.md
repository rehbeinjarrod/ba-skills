# scope-work

**Reach for it when** a piece of work is a sentence or two and you want it pinned down: its name, its goal, who decides, what it must achieve, what is left out and whether it needs several features. If you already hold notes or a document, use [capture-requirements](capture-requirements.md) first.

**Say** "Scope this work:" and a sentence or two about it. Later, "make the brief".

**What comes back first** is a draft scope written from what you said and three questions, each with a recommended answer. "Yes" takes the recommendation.

**How to tell it is working**

- Your records hold the work and its goal before any question is asked.
- Every question asked is recorded with its recommended answer, so an unanswered one is not lost.
- Each answer shows up in the next "Scope so far".

**It will not** ask more than three questions a turn, wait for every answer before making the Scope brief, or take a solution as the goal: it asks what result the solution is for.

**Good to know**

- Agreeing a scope and signing off are different things: "we will build this" against "this is what we are building". Sign-off comes later, through [prepare-signoff-brief](prepare-signoff-brief.md).
- This is where a work becomes Large: you say its goal needs several features.
- Asking to send the Scope brief is the yes: it goes without a second question.
