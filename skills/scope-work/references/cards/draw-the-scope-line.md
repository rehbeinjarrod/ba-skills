# Draw the scope line

When: the person cannot say what is left out, or two people expect different things from the work.

Steps:

1. Read the Goal aloud. For each thing asked for, ask: "Would the Goal still be met without this?"
2. A "yes" goes under `### Out of scope`, in their words, or stays with `Priority: could`.
3. Name three things a reader might expect and will not get: a neighbouring group of people, a neighbouring process, an exception. Ask yes or no for each.
4. Where they cannot decide, record a Question with who can.

Stop when: three "out" lines are agreed, or two in a row are answered "not sure".

Output: `### Out of scope` lines, priorities, Questions.
